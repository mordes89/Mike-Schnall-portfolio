01 / About

Good engineering starts with understanding.

I’m a Senior Solutions Engineer at Informed.IQ, helping enterprise clients integrate machine learning verification products into their systems.

My background spans military service, luxury travel operations, and software engineering. My move into tech began with a database solution that eliminated manual data entry. Seeing that impact sparked a passion for programming and led me through App Academy into enterprise SaaS.

Today, I combine my economics background with technical troubleshooting, data analysis, and cross-functional leadership. I work closely with clients throughout the full integration process, from understanding their needs and defining requirements to implementation and ongoing optimization, helping them get lasting value from the platform.
